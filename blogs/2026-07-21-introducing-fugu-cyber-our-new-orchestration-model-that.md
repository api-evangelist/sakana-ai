Today, we are releasing an update to our Fugu orchestration model: Fugu Cyber . Available as a new API endpoint , Fugu-Cyber is purpose-built for the complexities of modern cyber defense. Fugu-Cyber achieves state-of-the-art performance on the industry’s most challenging security benchmarks, reaching a success rate of 86.9% on CyberGym and 72.1% on CTI-REALM, comparable to leading cybersecurity-focused frontier models such as GPT-5.5-Cyber and Mythos-Preview.
